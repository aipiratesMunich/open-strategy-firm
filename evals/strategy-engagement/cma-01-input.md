# Kestrel Logistics: pharma cold-chain acquisition screen

*Fictional company, targets, people and figures, invented for evaluation.*

## Agreed with the CEO and CFO (September 2026, before any target discussion)

- **Strategic goal:** certified pharma cold-chain capability (GDP-certified, 2-8 degrees) in Kestrel's three home regions by 2028.
- **Build alternative:** internal estimate EUR 35m and about four years to certification and a customer base; misses 2028.
- **Budget:** up to EUR 65m enterprise value.
- **Must-haves:** GDP certification held today; revenue EUR 20m to 80m; operations in at least two of Kestrel's three home regions.
- **Deal-breakers:** any single customer above 30% of revenue; certification under review or lapsed; unresolved litigation with a customer.
- **Valuation convention (CFO):** value = next-year free cash flow / (10% - 2%). Cost synergies valued as run-rate / 10% less one-off cost, no phase-in. Revenue synergies are shown separately.

## Frostline Pharma Transport (banker teaser plus first management meeting)

- Asking price indicated by the banker: EUR 60m.
- Seller's plan: free cash flow EUR 4.6m next year (revenue growth 12% a year).
- History 2022-2025: revenue growth about 4% a year; Kestrel's haircut plan on that history gives free cash flow EUR 3.4m next year.
- The two largest contracts (18% and 9% of revenue) contain change-of-control clauses.
- The founder personally holds the relationship with the pharma quality auditors.
