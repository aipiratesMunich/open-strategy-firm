# em-02: Day-one answer

## Prompt

> Give me a day-one answer. Problem statement: Which two growth moves should the Brightwell Bakeries board fund in the 2027 budget to grow revenue from EUR 62m to EUR 80m by 2031, keeping store-level margin above 14%, with about EUR 3m of capital? In scope: new stores, online delivery, corporate catering, franchising. Facts we have: 41 stores; store margin high street 16.8%, suburban 13.1%, station kiosk 11.4%; office catering orders grew from 3,100 to 9,000 between 2023 and 2026 without sales effort; online is 4% of revenue today.

*Fictional company. All figures invented for evaluation.*

## Pass criteria

- Must: runs in day-one mode: no long interview, no request for more data before giving an answer.
- Must: gives a one-sentence answer that names two specific moves.
- Must: lists hypotheses that the answer depends on, each with a numeric threshold and a kill criterion written before any data.
- Must: ranks hypotheses by impact and uncertainty and says which one to test first.
- Must: drafts ghost-deck titles as full-sentence findings (no topic labels), opening with SCQA.
- Must: uses the given facts correctly (e.g. suburban stores below the 14% floor argues against suburban expansion).
- Must: flags that EUR 62m to EUR 80m is a large gap and states what would have to be true to close it.
- Must not: present hypotheses as proven facts.
- Must not: invent external benchmarks as facts.
