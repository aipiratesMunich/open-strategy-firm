# com-03: Messy interview notes and a proposed management layer

## Prompt

> These are my notes from 9 interviews about why our central bakery and stores don't work together. CEO wants regional managers. Make sense of it and tell me what we should change.

Input file: `com-03-input.md`

*Fictional company. All figures invented for evaluation.*

## What this case tests

Robustness. Unstructured, typo-ridden notes, no engagement folder, a structure already proposed. The skill should count what the notes actually say, separate examples from opinions, trace the symptom to process and decision rights, and prefer the smallest change over a new management layer.

## Reference values (counts across the 9 interviews)

- 2 pm cut-off or dropped late changes: 6 of 9 (I1, I2, I3, I4, I5, I9).
- Stores cannot see the production plan: 4 of 9 (I1, I3, I5, I8).
- No owner for substitutions or allocation when an item is short: 3 of 9 (I1, I4, I6).
- Culture or "they don't care" without an example: 2 of 9 (I2, I7).
- Support for regional managers: 2 of 9 (I5, I9).
- Waste nearly doubled: 4.8% to 9.2% (4.4 points), 4.2 points above the 5% target.

## Pass criteria

- Must: counts the themes in line with the reference values (a difference of one in a count is acceptable if the run explains its coding).
- Must: links the symptom (waste 4.8% to 9.2%, stockouts about 2 to 7) to the 2025 changes (central production, cut-off moved to 2 pm) as the main hypothesis, marked as a hypothesis to test with store-level data (offered by I8).
- Must: names the unowned decisions (late order changes after cut-off, substitutions and allocation when short, sharing the plan) and proposes explicit owners.
- Must: treats the culture statements (I2, I7) as opinions without examples and does not base the recommendation on them.
- Must: recommends process and decision-rights fixes first (e.g. share the production plan with stores, a later or per-store-type cut-off where production allows, a named owner for shortages) and keeps the regional manager layer as one option to judge after the fixes, with its cost and attention flagged.
- Must: states what data would confirm the diagnosis (store-level waste and stockouts before and after the 2025 change, timing of late changes).
- Must: answer line states what changes, in what sequence, by when.
- Must not: recommend the new regional layer as the main answer.
- Must not: invent quotes, interview counts or figures not in the notes.
