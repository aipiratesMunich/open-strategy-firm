# Ravensworth Industrial Seals: pricing data pack

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

**Question from the sales director:** "Can we raise list prices on the standard seal range by 5% from 1 March 2027? The board decides in January."

## Standard seal range, 2025 (Ravensworth finance)

- Pocket revenue (after all discounts, rebates and freight): EUR 24.0m.
- Contribution margin: 40% of pocket revenue (variable cost: material, direct labour, packaging).

## Competitor list prices (public price lists, September 2026)

For 40 comparable part numbers (same material, size and pressure rating), Ravensworth list prices sit 7% to 9% below both main competitors. Lead times are comparable; Ravensworth has the better on-time delivery record in customer audits (2025, 96% vs customers' stated 90% average).

## Past price change

List prices rose 3% in January 2023. Volume in the following 12 months fell 2%, against flat market volume (Ravensworth sales data; market volume from the industry association's shipment statistics as reported to members).

## Contracts

OEM key accounts are on annual contracts renewing in January; distributors on price lists that can change with 60 days' notice.
