# cg-01: Close a revenue gap with sized levers

## Prompt

> Work the Kirkby growth case from the briefing pack. Quick case, no engagement folder. I need the answer page for the CEO.

Input file: `cg-01-input.md`

*Fictional company. All figures invented for evaluation.*

## What this case tests

The happy path: a stated target, constraints, segment data, market growth, an installed base and a favoured adjacency without evidence. The skill should size the momentum case and the gap, split market growth from share change, size the core and installed base before the adjacency, show profit next to revenue, and write one answer page with kept and killed levers and the remaining gap.

## Reference values

- Segment CAGR 2023-2025: lab instruments about -1.2% a year; process sensors about +2.6%; service 0%.
- Momentum case 2030: lab about 75.2, sensors about 45.5, service 20.0, total about EUR 140.7m. Gap to EUR 175m: about EUR 34.3m.
- Market vs own growth 2023-2025: lab market +6.1% cumulative vs Kirkby -2.4% (share loss); sensors market +12.4% vs Kirkby +5.3% (share loss).
- Holding share from 2025 to 2030 instead of momentum: lab about 92.7 (+17.5), sensors about 53.5 (+8.1).
- Installed base: 7,700 contracts today (about EUR 10.0m). Raising coverage from 35% to 50% adds 3,300 contracts and about EUR 4.3m a year.
- Price: 3% on lab instruments is about EUR 2.4m on 2025 revenue (about EUR 2.3m on the 2030 momentum base) before any volume loss.
- Software: EUR 15m claimed with no evidence; it would be 44% of the gap.

## Pass criteria

- Must: states the decision question in the skill's form (gap from EUR 140m to 175m by 2030, EUR 15m capital, no acquisitions, EBIT margin at least 10%, board in March 2027).
- Must: computes a momentum case of about EUR 140 to 141m and a gap of about EUR 34m, using the CFO convention.
- Must: separates market growth from share change and states that Kirkby lost share in both lab instruments and process sensors.
- Must: sizes the core (share recovery, price) and installed base (service coverage) before the software adjacency, each with a base case, a range and an evidence grade.
- Must: installed-base lever about EUR 4.3m at 50% coverage (or another coverage level with consistent arithmetic).
- Must: treats the EUR 15m software figure as unsupported: widens the range or kills it pending evidence, and names the comparison that would settle it (e.g. comparable software launches by instrument makers, customer pilots) without inventing one.
- Must: shows profit next to revenue for every kept lever and checks the 10% EBIT floor.
- Must: shows the remaining gap openly after the base-case levers and says how it is closed or accepted.
- Must: the answer line names specific levers with amounts and investment, and a confidence level with one reason.
- Must: assumptions table and open-questions table in the skill's format.
- Must not: close the gap by stretching assumptions (e.g. full share recovery plus EUR 15m software at base case with no range).
- Must not: invent market data, competitor prices or benchmark ramp-up rates.
