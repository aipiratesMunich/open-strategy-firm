# Problem statement

*Fictional company. All figures invented for evaluation.*

Should the Marlow Garden Centres board approve EUR 4.2m in December 2026 to refurbish the cafés in all 12 centres from 2027, given the programme must pay back within four years and the board will not take on new debt?

Decision maker: the board, prepared by the CEO. Out of scope: opening new centres, selling the café business.
