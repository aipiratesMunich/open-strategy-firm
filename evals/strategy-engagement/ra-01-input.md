# Kestrel Logistics: same-day delivery for online retailers, evidence pack

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

## Branch from the workplan

- **Hypothesis (as written by the client team):** Online retailers will pay a premium for same-day delivery.
- **What it is for:** the board decides in December 2026 whether to roll same-day delivery out from the pilot city to four more cities (investment about EUR 3m). The business case needs a price per parcel and a take-up rate.
- **Kill criterion proposed by the client:** fewer than a quarter of the retailers we offer it to sign at EUR 4.50 per parcel or more.

## 2. Interview notes (Kestrel key-account team, 8 retailers, September 2026)

Eight retailers were interviewed, five of them already Kestrel same-day customers (R01 to R05), three non-customers from the pilot list (R07, R10, R13).

- Question asked: "Would you pay EUR 5 or more per parcel for reliable same-day delivery?"
- Six of eight answered yes. All five pilot customers answered yes; one of the three non-customers answered yes.
- R07 said it tested same-day with another carrier in 2025, saw 3% of orders choose it at checkout and stopped.
- R13 said it would come back "if the price were closer to EUR 3.50".
