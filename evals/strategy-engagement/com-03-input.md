# Brightwell Bakeries: raw interview notes, central bakery and stores

*Fictional company, people and figures, invented for evaluation. Notes typed by the COO's assistant, unedited.*

Context from COO: waste was 4.8% of production value in 2024, 9.2% Jan-Sep 2026, target 5%. Stockouts of top-10 items reported by stores up from ~2 to ~7 per store per week. In 2025 production moved to one central bakery and the cut-off for next-day store orders moved from 6 pm to 2 pm. CEO thinks we need a new layer of 4 regional managers between stores and HQ.

**I1, store manager Altstadt.** orders after 2pm just get dropped, no one tells us. we cant see what the bakery plans to bake tomorrow so we guess. when rye is short the driver decides what we get instead??

**I2, store manager Hafen.** cut-off at 2 is crazy, we know tomorrow's weather at 5. honestly the bakery people dont care about us.

**I3, production planner.** stores send changes at 4, 5 pm by phone, I can't put them in, plan is locked at 2. Stores don't see my plan sheet, it's on my drive. I'd share it if someone said I may.

**I4, head baker night shift.** late changes come in by whatsapp at night, I bake what the sheet says. when we run short of an item nobody tells me which store gets priority, I just split it.

**I5, store manager Bahnhof.** 2pm doesn't work for a station store, commuter numbers depend on the next day's timetable. don't know what the bakery is planning. a regional manager could fight for us.

**I6, logistics lead.** drivers decide substitutions on the ramp because nobody else does. not their job really.

**I7, store manager Uni.** its a culture thing, HQ never listens.

**I8, finance controller.** waste mainly in stores that order "safe" because they can't see production plan, can share the store-level numbers. plan sheet not in the ERP.

**I9, area sales.** biggest complaint I hear is the 2pm cut-off. regional managers would help, stores feel alone.
