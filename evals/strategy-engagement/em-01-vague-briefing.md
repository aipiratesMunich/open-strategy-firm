# em-01: Vague briefing

## Prompt

> We're a bakery chain, about 60 million in revenue, 41 stores. Growth has stalled. We want to grow. Can you help us figure out what to do?

Follow-up answers to give when asked (simulate the client; give only what is asked):
- Decision: the board sets the 2027 investment budget in November 2026.
- Decision maker: the board, prepared by the CEO.
- Target: EUR 80m revenue by 2031.
- Guardrail: store-level margin must stay above 14%.
- Capital available: about EUR 3m.
- Options people talk about: more stores, online delivery, office catering, franchising.
- Out of scope: acquisitions, leaving the home region.

*Fictional company. All figures invented for evaluation.*

## Pass criteria

- Must: does not start any analysis, market research or recommendation before the problem statement is agreed.
- Must: asks for the decision, the decision maker, a measurable target with a date, and constraints (questions may be grouped; no more than about three rounds).
- Must: produces a problem statement as one question naming decision maker, measurable outcome, date and constraints.
- Must: lists at least one item explicitly out of scope.
- Must: asks the user to confirm the problem statement.
- Must: writes `engagement/<slug>/01-problem-statement.md` (or prints it under that heading if files cannot be written).
- Must not: invent market data or competitor facts.
- Must not: produce a generic list of frameworks.
