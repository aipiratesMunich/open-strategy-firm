# Kirkby Analytical: growth briefing pack

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

**From the CEO:** "Revenue has been stuck at EUR 140m for three years. The board wants EUR 175m by 2030 and asked me to bring a growth plan to the March 2027 meeting. We have up to EUR 15m to invest, no acquisitions this cycle, and group EBIT margin must stay at 10% or above. Our head of strategy says the answer is process analytics software."

## Revenue and EBIT by segment (Kirkby finance)

| Segment | 2023 rev (EUR m) | 2024 | 2025 | EBIT margin 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lab instruments | 82 | 81 | 80 | 9% |
| Process sensors | 38 | 39 | 40 | 13% |
| Service (contracts, parts, repairs) | 20 | 20 | 20 | 24% |
| **Total** | **140** | **140** | **140** | **12.3%** |

**Momentum convention agreed with the CFO:** without new action, each segment continues its 2023-2025 compound growth rate to 2030.

## Installed base (Kirkby service CRM)

- 22,000 Kirkby instruments in the field.
- 35% are under a Kirkby service contract at an average EUR 1,300 per instrument per year.
- Service manager: "most of the rest are serviced by independent shops; some customers run without contracts."

## Price position (Kirkby sales team comparison of list prices for comparable lab instruments, Q2 2026)

Kirkby lab instruments list about 7% below the two main competitors for comparable specification. No volume reason is recorded for the gap.
