# Brightwell Bakeries: wholesale price response, sales director's proposal

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

**Proposal (sales director, October 2026):** "A competitor undercut us and we lost 8% of wholesale volume. I want to cut all wholesale prices to supermarkets by 10% from 1 November to win the volume back. It's the only language the chains understand."

## Wholesale business (Brightwell finance)

- Wholesale revenue to supermarkets, annualised after the competitor move: EUR 18.0m (before the move: about EUR 19.6m).
- Contribution margin on wholesale: 25% of revenue.

## What happened (Brightwell key account team)

- In June 2026 a regional industrial bakery cut its price by about 10% on two toast-bread lines at one supermarket chain (Chain A). Seen on the shelf and in Chain A's buyer feedback.
- Brightwell's two matching toast lines at Chain A fell from about EUR 2.6m to EUR 1.4m annualised revenue.
- The rest of Brightwell wholesale moved from about EUR 16.96m to EUR 16.6m (-2%), in line with the usual autumn dip of the past three years.
- No other chain has asked for a price change. Two chains renew their annual terms in March 2027.
