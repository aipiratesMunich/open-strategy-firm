# Saltmarsh Laundry Services: board note from the CEO

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

"Our EBIT margin fell from 16.7% in 2022 to 9.2% in 2025. Labour is our biggest cost, so I will propose a 15% headcount reduction to the board in November. I want the consultants to design the cut and confirm it gets us back to 15% margin by 2027."

## Contracts (commercial team)

- Hospital contracts (70% of volume): fixed price per kg, no energy or chemicals indexation. 40% of hospital volume renews in mid-2026, 60% in mid-2027.
- Hotel contracts: annual, repriced each January.
- Hospital contracts carry a 24-hour turnaround service level with penalties.

## Operations

- Plants run two shifts at about 94% of capacity. Kilograms washed per labour hour unchanged since 2022.

## Peers

Trade association margin survey 2025 (members' self-reported data): median EBIT margin of members with energy indexation in public contracts stayed between 14% and 16% from 2022 to 2025.
