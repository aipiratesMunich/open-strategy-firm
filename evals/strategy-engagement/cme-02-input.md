# Halvorsen Instruments: US entry, internal notes

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

## Slide from the corporate finance adviser (June 2026)

"The US laboratory instruments market is worth about USD 9bn. Capturing just 1% gives Halvorsen USD 90m in revenue."
No source given on the slide.

## Product team notes

- Halvorsen sells benchtop electrochemistry meters (pH, conductivity, dissolved oxygen) and nothing else. In the adviser's own appendix, benchtop electrochemistry is listed at about 2.5% of the US lab instruments total.
- Halvorsen meters carry EU certification only. Regulated US labs that buy calibrated instruments with a certified local calibration service will not buy without one. Halvorsen has no US calibration network. Product team estimate: about 20% of the US benchtop electrochemistry demand comes from labs that calibrate in-house and would accept this.
- One US distributor has offered a contract. It covers states that account for about 30% of US lab activity (distributor's own estimate).
- In Germany, Halvorsen took 30% of its addressable benchtop segment over twelve years, its best result anywhere.

## Board ask

"Minimum USD 10m revenue by 2030 to be worth the effort. Up to USD 4m to invest."
