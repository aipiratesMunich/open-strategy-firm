# cg-03: Minimal briefing, standalone

## Prompt

> Pellworth Textiles. Revenue 31m in 2026, flat for years. Board wants 40m by 2029. No M&A. That's all I've got right now, what's your take?

*Fictional company. All figures invented for evaluation.*

## What this case tests

Robustness. Almost no data, no engagement folder. The skill should ask only what the user can answer (in small groups), not stall, size the gap, build a gap-led issue tree with kill criteria, and give a provisional answer clearly marked as hypotheses, with open questions. It must not fill the void with invented market data.

## Reference values

- Gap: EUR 9m, 29% above 2026 revenue.
- Required growth: (40 / 31)^(1/3) - 1 = about 8.9% a year for three years, against a flat history.

## Pass criteria

- Must: computes the gap (EUR 9m, about 29%) and the required growth rate (about 9% a year from 2026 to 2029), and contrasts it with the flat history.
- Must: asks a short, grouped set of questions the user can answer (e.g. revenue by product, customer and region; margin floor; capital available; who decides and when; whether the market is flat or share is lost), no more than about three rounds, and proceeds without waiting if the user has no more data.
- Must: produces an issue tree that starts from the gap, with branches for momentum, core (market vs share, price, existing customers), adjacencies and portfolio, each with a hypothesis and a kill criterion.
- Must: keeps inorganic moves out of scope or names them as ruled out by the board.
- Must: states the analysis order (momentum case and market vs share split first).
- Must: gives a provisional answer or the most likely shape of the answer, explicitly labelled as hypotheses, with confidence low and the reason.
- Must: open-questions table in the skill's format (question, why it matters, who can answer, effect on the answer).
- Must not: invent textile market growth rates, competitor names, or benchmark figures as facts.
- Must not: deliver a generic list of growth frameworks or ideas unconnected to the EUR 9m gap.
