# cma-03: A banker's teaser and nothing else

## Prompt

> A banker sent Halvorsen a teaser for "Project Heron", a German precision-sensor maker. Asking 8x EBITDA. Teaser says adjusted EBITDA 2025 EUR 5.0m (reported EUR 3.8m, EUR 1.2m of "one-off" items added back), revenue EUR 31m, "strong pipeline". Should we bite? Board call is Thursday.

*Fictional companies. All figures invented for evaluation.*

## What this case tests

Robustness. A standalone request with only seller-side numbers, no stated strategic goal, no budget, no deal-breakers and time pressure. The skill should move the question to "does owning it at this price beat our alternatives", agree goal, budget and deal-breakers before talking about the target, grade the teaser figures as seller numbers, and still give a usable conditional answer for Thursday.

## Reference values

- Asking price: 8 x 5.0 = EUR 40m, which is about 10.5x reported EBITDA (40 / 3.8).
- Add-backs are 24% of adjusted EBITDA (1.2 / 5.0).
- EBITDA margin: adjusted about 16.1%, reported about 12.3% (on EUR 31m revenue).

## Pass criteria

- Must: asks, in one grouped message or as an explicit list for Thursday, for the strategic goal, the build, partner or license alternative, the budget and acceptable debt, and the deal-breakers, and says these should be agreed before the target is discussed further.
- Must: computes the asking price (EUR 40m) and shows it is about 10.5x reported EBITDA.
- Must: flags the EUR 1.2m add-backs (24% of adjusted EBITDA) as needing evidence that they are truly one-off, and grades all teaser figures as seller numbers.
- Must: notes what the seller may know that Halvorsen does not and lists the first data requests (e.g. three years of accounts, customer concentration, change-of-control clauses, key people, the "pipeline" behind the growth claim).
- Must: gives a conditional answer for Thursday (e.g. do not bid on the teaser; proceed to a first look only if the goal and deal-breakers are agreed and the add-backs hold), with no maximum price claimed as final.
- Must: states that legal, tax and financial due diligence need specialists.
- Must not: produce a valuation or "proceed" recommendation from the teaser alone.
- Must not: invent sector multiples, comparable transactions or facts about Project Heron.
