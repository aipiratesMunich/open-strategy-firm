# Kestrel Logistics: parcel division margin decline, data pack

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

**From the CFO:** "Parcel division EBIT margin fell from 8.0% in 2023 to 2.5% in 2025. The board wants at least 6% back by 2027 and a plan at the February 2027 meeting. We have not picked a cure."

## Division P&L drivers (Kestrel finance)

| Driver | 2023 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Parcels (m) | 50.0 | 52.0 |
| Revenue per parcel (EUR) | 4.00 | 4.05 |
| Variable cost per parcel (EUR) | 2.90 | 3.18 |
| of which labour (drivers, sorters) | 1.60 | 1.78 |
| of which fuel | 0.70 | 0.78 |
| of which subcontractors | 0.60 | 0.62 |
| Fixed cost (EUR m) | 39.0 | 40.0 |

## Labour detail (HR and operations)

- Wage agreement: +9% total from 2023 to 2025.
- Parcels per driver hour: 18.0 in 2023, 17.6 in 2025.

## Contracts (commercial team)

- 60% of 2025 volume is on three-year contracts signed in early 2023 at a fixed EUR 4.00 per parcel, without fuel or wage indexation. They renew in the first half of 2026.
- 40% of volume is on annual contracts, repriced to about EUR 4.125 by 2025.

## Balance sheet and covenants (treasury)

- Net debt EUR 44m. 2025 EBITDA EUR 14.2m (EBIT 5.2m plus depreciation 9.0m).
- Bank covenant: net debt / EBITDA at most 3.5x. Cash covers about nine months of fixed cost.

## Peers

The CFO says two listed competitors "held their margins" in 2024 and 2025, based on their annual reports; figures not compiled yet.
