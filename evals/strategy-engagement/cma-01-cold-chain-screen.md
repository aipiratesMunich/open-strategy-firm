# cma-01: Screen a long list and set a walk-away price

## Prompt

> Run the Kestrel cold-chain M&A screen from the pack and tell us whether to go ahead with Frostline at the asking price.

Input file: `cma-01-input.md`

*Fictional companies. All figures invented for evaluation.*

## What this case tests

The happy path: goal, build alternative, must-haves, deal-breakers and valuation convention agreed upfront; a long list including companies not for sale; a preferred target with a seller plan and a history; named cost synergies and an unowned revenue synergy. The skill should run the funnel on the full field, value the target from the buyer's view on the haircut plan, add only net cost synergies to the walk-away price and give proceed, do not proceed or proceed under conditions with the maximum price.

## Reference values

- Must-haves remove ColdArc (size), Icepath (not certified), Glacier Freight (one region), Chillbox (size). Deal-breaker removes Polar Route (41% top customer). Remaining: Frostline, Medivan Logistik, Thermo Lane. Two of the three are not known to be for sale.
- Standalone value: haircut plan 3.4 / 0.08 = EUR 42.5m; seller plan 4.6 / 0.08 = EUR 57.5m.
- Net cost synergies: (1.2 + 0.5) / 0.10 - (2.0 + 0.2) = 17.0 - 2.2 = EUR 14.8m.
- Walk-away price: 42.5 + 14.8 = EUR 57.3m, against an asking price of EUR 60m (EUR 2.7m above).
- Revenue synergy as claimed: 2.0 / 0.10 = EUR 20m of value, upside only, outside the walk-away price.

## Pass criteria

- Must: runs the funnel on all eight companies with the stage at which each drops out, and keeps Medivan Logistik and Thermo Lane on the short list although they are not known to be for sale.
- Must: values Frostline on the haircut plan (about EUR 42.5m) and shows the seller plan (about EUR 57.5m) as the seller's view, graded as seller numbers.
- Must: net cost synergies of about EUR 14.8m, each with owner, date and one-off cost.
- Must: walk-away price of about EUR 57.3m, built from standalone value plus net cost synergies, and states that EUR 60m is above it.
- Must: shows the EUR 2.0m revenue synergy as upside only, outside the walk-away, and notes it has no owner, date or one-off cost.
- Must: answer is do not proceed at EUR 60m, or proceed only at or below about EUR 57m under named conditions (e.g. change-of-control consents for the 18% and 9% contracts, founder retention for the auditor relationship).
- Must: names the change-of-control clauses and founder dependence as deal-breaker checks or conditions.
- Must: compares the deal with the build route (EUR 35m, four years, misses 2028).
- Must: recommends a review by someone outside the deal team.
- Must: flags legal, tax and financial due diligence for specialists.
- Must not: include revenue synergies in the walk-away price or back-solve value from the asking price.
- Must not: invent facts about the targets beyond the table (e.g. margins, customers, owners).
