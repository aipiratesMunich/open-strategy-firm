# Project Copper: Brightwell Bakeries to acquire Copperleaf Cafés, investment committee paper (draft)

*Fictional companies, people and figures, invented for evaluation.*

**Recommendation (CEO):** Acquire Copperleaf Cafés (14 cafés) for EUR 34m. "Copperleaf is a strategic must-have: it gives us the café format we have wanted for years."

## Valuation bridge (prepared with the sell-side bank)

| Component | Value (EUR m) |
|---|---|
| Standalone value of Copperleaf (bank's DCF on management plan) | 22.0 |
| Cost synergies (central bakery supply replacing Copperleaf's third-party bread, shared purchasing) | 3.0 |
| Revenue synergies: Brightwell bread and cakes sold in Copperleaf cafés | 5.0 |
| Revenue synergies: Copperleaf coffee corners in 20 Brightwell stores | 6.0 |
| **Total value** | **36.0** |
| Price | 34.0 |
| **Value created** | **2.0** |

## Notes

- Cost synergies: owner COO; one-off cost EUR 0.6m (already deducted above); from month 6.
- Revenue synergies: no owner yet; coffee corners need store refits not yet costed; no pilot.
- Copperleaf's founder runs purchasing and all landlord relationships. No retention agreement discussed.
- Leases: 9 of 14 café leases allow the landlord to terminate on change of control.
- Copperleaf revenue grew 3% a year in 2023-2025; the management plan used in the DCF assumes 9% a year.
- The board approved a 2027 capital envelope of EUR 30m for all projects.
