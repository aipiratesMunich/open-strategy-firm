# cme-01: Market entry with a bottom-up ramp and an alternative use of capital

## Prompt

> Work the Kestrel Czech entry case from the briefing. Quick case, answer page for the board.

Input file: `cme-01-input.md`

*Fictional company. All figures invented for evaluation.*

## What this case tests

The happy path: a minimum bar, a market study with top-down and bottom-up numbers, a channel-capacity ramp from the client's own data, an incumbent with a known reaction, a sunk study, and an alternative use of capital. The skill should size the serviceable segment both ways, build share from sales capacity, model the incumbent reaction, compare with the alternative, write exit criteria, and give enter, do not enter, or enter later under conditions.

## Reference values

- Top-down medical segment: 210 x 15% = EUR 31.5m. Bottom-up pharmacies: 2,700 x 9,500 = about EUR 25.7m. The gap is plausibly hospitals and labs, which the bottom-up excludes.
- Ramp without churn: 120 new accounts a year, 480 by end of 2030, revenue about EUR 4.56m (about 18% of the pharmacy segment). With 8% churn: about 425 accounts, about EUR 4.0m.
- 2030 EBIT without churn: 4.56 x 30% - 0.9 - 3 x 0.085 = about EUR 0.21m (about 4.7% margin). With churn about EUR 0.06m (about 1.4%).
- Reaching EUR 6m needs about 632 accounts by 2030, about 158 a year, about 4 reps at the Austrian win rate.
- Incumbent 10% price cut on the no-churn case: revenue about EUR 4.1m, EBIT about EUR -0.24m.
- Alternative: EUR 2m in Austria for about EUR 0.35m EBIT by 2030 (management estimate).

## Pass criteria

- Must: states the decision question with the minimum bar (EUR 6m, 8% EBIT by 2030, EUR 5m) and kill criteria before analysing.
- Must: sizes the segment top-down (about EUR 31.5m) and bottom-up (about EUR 25.7m) and explains or flags the difference.
- Must: builds the ramp from reps x win rate (not "x% of the market") and arrives at about EUR 4.0m to 4.6m revenue in 2030, below the EUR 6m bar.
- Must: computes 2030 EBIT margin below 8% (about 1% to 5%).
- Must: models at least one aggressive incumbent reaction using the 2024 price-cut evidence and reports its effect.
- Must: states that the EUR 120k study is sunk and plays no role.
- Must: compares with the Austrian alternative per euro of capital, and flags that alternative as a management estimate to test.
- Must: does not approve the briefed 3-rep plan; answer is do not enter, enter later, or enter only under named conditions (e.g. a larger sales team, proof of win rate in a pilot region, a distributor or partner mode) with a stage gate before full capital is committed, and says what would make the board revisit it.
- Must: compares at least two entry modes (e.g. own office, partner or distributor, acquisition of a local courier) on cost, speed, control and exit cost.
- Must: writes exit criteria and exit cost.
- Must: flags that Austrian win rates and prices may not transfer to the Czech market and names the test.
- Must not: recommend unconditional entry on the strength of the EUR 210m headline.
- Must not: invent Czech regulatory requirements, competitor names or wage levels as facts; it may flag them for specialists.
