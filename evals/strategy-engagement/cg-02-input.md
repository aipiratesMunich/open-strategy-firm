# Corvina Coffee Roasters: growth plan 2026-2029 (management draft)

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

**Target set by the investors:** EUR 75m revenue by 2029. EBIT margin at least 8%. Up to EUR 6m of investment.

## Our core is healthy

Our wholesale coffee business (roasted coffee to cafés, restaurants and offices) grew from EUR 48m in 2023 to EUR 52m in 2025. That is solid growth in a competitive market. We plan the core to continue at 4% a year.

Market data: the national wholesale coffee market was EUR 600m in 2023 and has grown 9% a year since (roasters' trade association yearbook 2025, as quoted in our investor update).

## The growth engine: Corvina café franchise

We will open franchised Corvina cafés from 2027. Plan: 0 cafés in 2026, 25 in 2027, 60 in 2028, 110 in 2029, giving EUR 14m revenue to Corvina in 2029 (royalties plus coffee supply). Investment EUR 4.5m (franchise team, brand, flagship). No franchise operator has been signed yet.

## Plan summary

| EUR m | 2025 | 2029 |
|---|---|---|
| Core wholesale | 52.0 | 60.8 |
| Café franchise | 0 | 14.0 |
| **Total** | **52.0** | **74.8** |

Remaining gap to target: none.
