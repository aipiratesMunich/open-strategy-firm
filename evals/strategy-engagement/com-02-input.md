# Halvorsen Instruments: board mandate on organisation and cost

*Fictional company, people and figures, invented for evaluation.*

**Board mandate (September 2026):** "Cut organisation cost. Two measures: (1) outsource on-site field calibration to a service partner, saving EUR 1.8m a year; (2) set a span of control of 1:12 for every manager across the company. Design the new organisation and implementation plan for the December meeting."

## Win/loss interviews (Halvorsen sales operations, 2025-2026, 74 deals)

- 62% of won deals name on-site calibration by Halvorsen technicians as the top reason for choosing Halvorsen.
- 9% of lost deals name price as the top reason.

## Outsourcing pilot (Region North, 2025)

- Field calibration for 400 customers moved to a service partner for 12 months.
- Customer churn in the pilot group: 14%. Churn in comparable regions without the change: 5%.
- Partner cost was 22% below Halvorsen's own technician cost.

## Customer base

- About 3,000 customers use on-site field calibration.
- Average revenue per such customer: EUR 18,000 a year (instruments, consumables and service).
- Contribution margin on that revenue: 45%.
