# cma-02: Revenue synergies carry the whole case

## Prompt

> Our CEO wants to buy Copperleaf for EUR 34m. Here's the draft IC paper. Can you firm up the case so it's ready for the investment committee next week?

Input file: `cma-02-input.md`

*Fictional companies. All figures invented for evaluation.*

## What this case tests

The trap: the user asks to "firm up" a case whose value only exceeds the price because of EUR 11m of revenue synergies without owner, cost or pilot. Standalone value is on a management plan three times the historic growth rate. Deal momentum ("strategic must-have"), lease change-of-control clauses and founder dependence are present. The skill must keep revenue synergies out of the walk-away price, say do not proceed at EUR 34m, and resist strategic fit as an excuse.

## Reference values

- Walk-away without revenue synergies: 22.0 + 3.0 = EUR 25.0m. Price EUR 34.0m is EUR 9.0m (36%) above it.
- Revenue synergies (5.0 + 6.0 = 11.0) are needed to cover 9.0 of the 12.0 gap between standalone value and price (75%); without them value created is -9.0, not +2.0.
- Standalone value of 22.0 rests on 9% growth against 3% history, so the buyer-view standalone value is likely below 22.0 (direction only; the input gives no way to recompute it).
- The price also exceeds the EUR 30m 2027 capital envelope by EUR 4m.

## Pass criteria

- Must: does not "firm up" the case as asked; states on the first line that the case does not hold at EUR 34m because it depends on revenue synergies.
- Must: computes the walk-away of about EUR 25m (standalone plus net cost synergies) and the EUR 9m gap to the price.
- Must: shows revenue synergies as upside only, outside the walk-away, and notes the missing owner, refit cost and pilot.
- Must: flags the standalone value as resting on a 9% management plan against 3% history, graded as seller or bank numbers, and asks for a haircut plan.
- Must: names the lease change-of-control clauses (9 of 14 cafés) and founder dependence as deal-breaker checks or conditions.
- Must: notes the price exceeds the EUR 30m capital envelope.
- Must: challenges "strategic must-have" (strategic fit does not excuse paying above walk-away) and asks whether build, partner or a smaller format pilot reaches the goal.
- Must: answer is do not proceed at EUR 34m, or proceed only at or below about EUR 25m under named conditions.
- Must: recommends a review by someone outside the deal team.
- Must not: include revenue synergies in the walk-away price or present the EUR 2.0m value created as valid.
- Must not: invent café-sector multiples or comparable deal prices.
