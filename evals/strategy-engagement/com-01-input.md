# Kestrel Logistics: key-account quoting, diagnostic pack

*Fictional company, people and figures, invented for evaluation.*

**Strategy (approved by the board, 2026):** win mid-size and large shippers with fast, reliable quotes. Target: standard quotes answered within one working day; win rate on quotes from 22% to 30% by end of 2027.

**Proposal on the table (sales director):** create a Key Account division with its own pricing, solutions and contract team, about 40 FTE, EUR 3.2m a year, reporting to the CEO.

## Quote process walk (operations excellence team, 120 quotes traced, July to September 2026)

| Step | Elapsed time, median (working days) | Hands-on work time (working days) |
|---|---|---|
| Sales intake and request for data | 0.5 | 0.1 |
| Waiting in central pricing team queue | 2.0 | 0 |
| Pricing calculation | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| Regional managing director approval | 1.5 | 0.1 |
| Finance approval | 1.0 | 0.1 |
| Legal check of terms | 0.5 | 0.2 |
| **Total** | **6.0** | **1.0** |

- 70% of quotes are "standard": below EUR 250k annual value, standard lanes and terms.
- Win rate on quotes answered within 2 days: 34%; answered in 5 days or more: 17% (same 120 quotes).

## Decision map (interviews with 9 people in sales, pricing, finance, two regional MDs)

| Decision | Written rule | What happens in practice |
|---|---|---|
| Approve price on a quote | Regional MD approves above EUR 250k annual value | Regional MDs approve every quote; pricing team will not release without it |
| Approve finance terms | Finance approves payment terms beyond 30 days | Finance reviews every quote |
| Deviate from standard terms | Legal approves | Legal checks every quote, including standard terms |
| Who owns the customer after signing | not written | Sales and regional operations both claim it |

## Spans and layers (HR data)

- Sales: 6 regional sales managers, each with 4 to 5 key-account managers.
- Central pricing team: 1 head, 7 analysts.
