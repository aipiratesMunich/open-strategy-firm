# Brightwell Bakeries: Growth Strategy 2027-2031

*Fictional company. All figures invented for evaluation.*

**Problem statement (agreed with the CEO, September 2026):** Which two growth moves should the Brightwell board fund in the 2027 budget to grow revenue from EUR 62m to EUR 80m by 2031, keeping store-level margin above 14%? Options in scope: new stores, online delivery, corporate catering, franchising.

---

## Executive summary

Brightwell is a strong regional brand with loyal customers. Our analysis of the market shows significant potential in several areas. Customers love the brand: 92% of surveyed customers would recommend Brightwell to a friend.

Corporate catering can add EUR 9m in revenue by 2031. Online delivery is growing fast across the industry and can reach EUR 11m if we grow 35% per year. New stores remain an option in suburban locations.

**We recommend exploring the catering opportunity further and considering an accelerated online roadmap.** Next steps will be defined with management.
