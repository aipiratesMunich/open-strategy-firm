# Harrow Cold Chain: second distribution hub, board deck (draft v3)

*Fictional company. All figures and people are invented.*

---

**Slide 1: Build the Leipzig hub in 2027: EUR 14.0m capex pays back in 3.1 years and frees the Kassel site from overtime**

Recommendation: the board approves EUR 14.0m capex for a second hub in Leipzig, construction start Q2 2027, operations from Q3 2028. Owner: COO Petra Lindqvist.

- Kassel runs at 96% of capacity; overtime cost EUR 2.3m in 2025
- Leipzig cuts average delivery distance to eastern customers by 41%
- Payback 3.1 years, NPV EUR 9.8m at 8% discount rate
- Downside (volume 15% below plan): payback 4.4 years, NPV still positive

---

**Slide 2: Kassel is full: utilisation passed 90% in 2024 and overtime has tripled since 2022**

| Year | Utilisation | Overtime cost |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 78% | EUR 0.7m |
| 2023 | 86% | EUR 1.2m |
| 2024 | 92% | EUR 1.8m |
| 2025 | 96% | EUR 2.3m |

Source: Harrow WMS and payroll, extracted 2026-08-14.

---

**Slide 3: Eastern customers drive the growth: 62% of new volume since 2023 ships east of Kassel**

New volume 2023 to 2025: 118,000 pallets, of which 73,000 to customers in Saxony, Thuringia and Brandenburg. Source: Harrow order data 2023 to 2025.

---

**Slide 4: Three options were tested; only Leipzig removes the capacity limit for ten years**

| Option | Capex | Capacity added | Limit reached |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extend Kassel (third shift + racking) | EUR 3.2m | 12% | 2028 |
| Third-party warehouse in Halle | EUR 0.4m + rent | 20% | 2030 |
| Own hub Leipzig | see slide 6 | 55% | after 2036 |

The third-party option was rejected because two of five large customers require GDP-certified temperature control that the Halle operator does not hold (customer contracts, clause 7.2).

---

**Slide 5: Leipzig cuts eastern delivery distance by 41% and saves EUR 1.1m transport cost a year**

Average distance to eastern customers: 312 km from Kassel, 184 km from Leipzig. Transport cost model based on 2025 carrier rates (EUR 1.62 per pallet-km bracket).

**Slide 8: Payback of 3.1 years; NPV of EUR 9.8m**

Cash flows: capex in 2027 and 2028, savings from 2029, 10-year horizon, 8% discount rate. Model: `harrow_leipzig_v3.xlsx`, sheet "Case".

---

**Slide 9: Even with 15% less volume, the hub pays back in 4.4 years**

| Scenario | Volume vs plan | Payback | NPV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | 100% | 3.1 years | EUR 9.8m |
| Downside | 85% | 4.4 years | EUR 4.1m |
| Upside | 110% | 2.7 years | EUR 12.6m |

The answer flips only if volume falls below 70% of plan, which would mean losing two of the five largest customers.
