# Kestrel Logistics: cross-dock hub business case, inputs

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

**Decision:** Should Kestrel invest in a cross-dock hub in the south region? The CFO's rule: approve if NPV at the 9% hurdle rate is above zero over the hub's eight-year life.

**Conventions agreed with finance:** EUR m; investment paid at the start of 2027 (t = 0); operating cash flows at the end of each year 2027 to 2034, discounted 1 to 8 years; no terminal value; no tax in this screening model.

| Hub operating fixed cost | 0.9 | 0.7 | 1.2 | EUR m per year | Site lease offer plus 14 FTE staffing plan |
| Investment (building fit-out, sorter) | 6.0 | 5.5 | 7.5 | EUR m | Two supplier quotes, September 2026 |
| Hurdle rate | 9% | 7% | 11% | per year | Group finance |

**Planning team note:** "If a competitor opens its own hub nearby, some shippers move and we would route fewer parcels; they would also force a price response that eats part of the saving, and the fit-out would cost more because we would accelerate it."
