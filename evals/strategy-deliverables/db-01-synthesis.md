# 05-synthesis: Fernhill Fitness, growth moves for 2027-2028

*Fictional company. All figures, people and documents invented for evaluation.*

**Problem statement:** Which growth moves should the Fernhill Fitness board approve on 10 December 2026 to lift revenue from EUR 58.0m (2026) to EUR 66.0m by 2028 without opening new gyms in new cities and with at most EUR 2m of investment?

**Governing thought:** Fernhill should fill its empty daytime capacity with an off-peak membership, sell corporate wellness contracts and raise the premium tier price by EUR 4; together they add about EUR 6.7m by 2028 and leave a EUR 1.3m gap that the board should accept for now rather than chase with a new city.

## Key line

1. **Daytime capacity is the largest unused asset.** Gyms run at 92% utilisation from 6 to 9 pm but 31% from 10 am to 4 pm. An off-peak membership at EUR 24 a month, priced below the standard EUR 39, reaches about 10,800 members by 2028 and adds about EUR 3.1m a year (range EUR 2.0m to 3.8m). Source: gate-entry data, 34 gyms, Jan to Aug 2026; off-peak pilot in 4 gyms, May to Aug 2026 (420 sign-ups, 6% from existing members downgrading); Fernhill growth model v4.
2. **Corporate wellness contracts are proven in the pilot.** Six employers signed in the pilot (1,150 employees enrolled). At 40 contracts by 2028 at an average EUR 60k a year, revenue adds about EUR 2.4m (range EUR 1.4m to 3.0m). Needs two corporate sales hires. Source: corporate pilot contracts 2026; Fernhill growth model v4.
3. **The premium tier is priced below both main competitors.** Fernhill premium costs EUR 59 a month against EUR 64 and EUR 67 at the two nearest competitors for a comparable offer (pool, classes, sauna). A EUR 4 increase on 25,000 premium members adds about EUR 1.2m a year before churn; at the churn seen after the 2024 increase (1.5 points extra for three months) about EUR 1.1m. Source: competitor public price lists, September 2026; Fernhill membership data 2024-2026; Fernhill growth model v4.
4. **A new city does not pay back within the board's four-year limit.** A gym in a new city needs EUR 4.5m and pays back in about seven years at the ramp of the last three openings. Source: opening P&Ls 2019-2023; Fernhill growth model v4.

| Step | EUR m |

## Decision requested

Approve EUR 1.9m (off-peak launch marketing EUR 0.8m, two corporate sales hires and pilot extension EUR 0.6m, booking-app changes EUR 0.5m). Owner: COO Lena Marsh. Start January 2027. Premium price change from 1 March 2027.

## Risks

- Off-peak cannibalisation: if more than 15% of new off-peak members are downgrading standard members, the off-peak move adds less than EUR 2.0m.
- Corporate sales ramp depends on two hires starting by February 2027.

## What would change the answer

Off-peak take-up below 6,000 members by end of 2027.
