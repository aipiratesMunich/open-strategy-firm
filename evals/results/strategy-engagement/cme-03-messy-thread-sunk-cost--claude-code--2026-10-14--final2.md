**Confidence:** Low-medium, because the case rests on one unverified management estimate (Tomasz's "80m+"), one commissioned study we cannot read (PL_study_final_v3.pdf wasn't attached), and zero Poland-specific cost, margin or competitor data. The revenue target's feasibility is low confidence until the open questions below are closed.

**Specialists and review:** Polish commercial/distribution law for the distributor agreements (exclusivity, termination, agency-law exposure) and HR/employment law for Tomasz's contract terms if the exit checkpoint below is ever triggered. Not assessed here.
