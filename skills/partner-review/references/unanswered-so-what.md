# Unanswered So-What

## What it catches

Content that is true, sourced and well drawn, and still useless because it never says what it means for the decision. Fact-reporting pages, descriptive titles, recommendations without action. This is the most common reason a client says "interesting" and does nothing.

## When to apply

- Every page title, the executive summary and every recommendation at gate 3.
- The synthesis before slides are built ([Synthesis](synthesis.md)).

## Checks

1. **Title states an implication.** Does the page title say what the data means for the client, not only what the data is?
2. **Implication reaches the decision.** Following the chain "so what?" from the finding, does it arrive at the decision in the problem statement within two or three steps?
3. **Action is concrete.** Does each recommendation name who, what, by when, and the resources?
4. **Magnitude given.** Is it clear how big the effect is (euros, percent, customers) so the client can prioritise?
5. **Comparison present.** Is the number compared with something (target, competitor, last year, cost of the alternative), so the reader can judge it?
6. **Orphan content.** Are there pages or paragraphs that do not support any key-line statement? They belong in the appendix or nowhere.
7. **Ending.** Does the document end with next steps and a decision request rather than a summary of findings?

## Severity

- **Blocker:** check 3 on the main recommendation; check 2 on the governing thought.
- **Major:** checks 1, 2, 4 on key-line pages; check 7.
- **Minor:** checks 5, 6.

## False alarms

- Context pages (a short situation page at the start) can be descriptive if the title still frames why the context matters.
- Appendix pages may report facts without implications; they exist for reference.
- Not every recommendation needs a full budget at gate 2; at gate 3 the main one does.

## Worked example

*Fictional example. Company, figures and people are invented.*

Page title: "Installed base by age band." Body: bar chart of Alder's 60,000 pumps. Review:

- Major (check 1): Title is a label. Rewrite: "More than half of the installed pumps are older than eight years and uncovered by any service contract: a EUR 31m annual revenue pool."
- Major (check 2): Link it to the decision on the next page: "...which makes service contracts the largest of the growth moves the board can fund."
- Minor (check 5): Add a comparison: share of uncovered pumps at the two main competitors, if known, or the trend since 2020.

## Output template

```markdown
# So-what review: <document>

| # | Severity | Where | Current title / statement | So-what chain (finding → implication → decision) | Rewrite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Pages to move to appendix:**
**Main recommendation has who/what/when/resources:** yes / no
```
