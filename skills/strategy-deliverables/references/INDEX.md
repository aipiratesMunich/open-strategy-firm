# References

Load only what the current step needs.

| Reference | Type | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| [Business Case](business-case.md) | analysis | A cash-flow comparison of the world with a decision and the world without it, over a defined horizon, valued with NPV, IRR and payback, and tested in base, down and up cases. |
| [Cost Benchmarking](cost-benchmarking.md) | analysis | A comparison of a cost line against a reference: other units of the same company, competitors, or industry data. |
| [Market Sizing](market-sizing.md) | analysis | An estimate of how much revenue a defined market holds, built twice: once top-down from a published aggregate, once bottom-up from countable units times value per unit. |
| [Pricing Analysis](pricing-analysis.md) | analysis | A structured test of where a price should sit and what a price change does to profit. |
| [Scenario Planning](scenario-planning.md) | analysis | A set of two to four internally consistent descriptions of how the client's environment could look at the decision horizon, built from the few uncertainties that matter most. |
| [Unit Economics](unit-economics.md) | analysis | The profit of one unit of business: one product sold, one contract-year, one customer. |
| [Model Red Flags](model-red-flags.md) | critique | Financial models that produce wrong numbers, or right numbers nobody can verify. |
| [Partner Review Checklist](partner-review-checklist.md) | critique | Everything that would embarrass the team in front of the client: a question that was never answered, conclusions that do not follow, numbers that do not tie, evidence that would not survive one sharp question, slides that bury the message. |
| [Slide Quality Red Flags](slide-quality-red-flags.md) | critique | Slides that hide their message: titles that describe instead of conclude, charts that do not prove the title, decoration that competes with data, and inconsistencies that make the reader doubt the numbers. |
| [Unanswered So-What](unanswered-so-what.md) | critique | Content that is true, sourced and well drawn, and still useless because it never says what it means for the decision. |
| [Action Titles](action-titles.md) | deliverable | The title of a slide written as the conclusion the slide proves, in one full sentence. |
| [Chart Selection](chart-selection.md) | deliverable | Choosing the chart from the message, not from the data. |
| [Deck Structure](deck-structure.md) | deliverable | The order and size of a recommendation deck: answer first, then the key line in sections, then next steps, then the appendix. |
| [Excel Model Standards](excel-model-standards.md) | deliverable | The build rules for any numbers model behind a recommendation: business case, market sizing, pricing effect, scenario model. |
| [Executive Summary](executive-summary.md) | deliverable | One page that gives the complete answer: the recommendation, the reasons, the size of the prize, the risks and the decision requested. |
| [One-Page Memo](one-page-memo.md) | deliverable | A single page of prose that states an answer, the reasons and the next step, written to be read without a meeting. |
| [Slide Anatomy](slide-anatomy.md) | deliverable | The fixed set of zones every content slide has, and what each zone may contain. |
| [Profit Tree](profit-tree.md) | framework | A logic tree that breaks profit into revenue minus cost, revenue into volume times price, and cost into fixed and variable parts, then further down to drivers that can be measured and influenced. |
| [SWOT](swot.md) | framework | A two-by-two summary of internal strengths and weaknesses and external opportunities and threats. |
| [Value Chain](value-chain.md) | framework | A breakdown of a firm into the activities that create its product and support it: inbound logistics, operations, outbound logistics, marketing and sales, service, plus support activities such as procurement, technology, people and infrastructure. |
| [Eighty-Twenty](eighty-twenty.md) | method | A prioritisation heuristic: in many business distributions a small share of items (customers, products, causes, leaves of a tree) accounts for a large share of the effect. |
| [MECE](mece.md) | method | A quality test for any list that breaks a whole into parts: the parts should be mutually exclusive (no item counted twice) and collectively exhaustive (nothing material left out). |
| [Pyramid Principle](pyramid-principle.md) | method | A way to order thinking and writing so the reader gets the answer first and the support second. |
| [SCQA](scqa.md) | method | A four-part opening for a document or presentation: Situation, Complication, Question, Answer. |
| [Sensitivity Analysis](sensitivity-analysis.md) | method | Changing a model's inputs to see how much the output moves, so the team knows which assumptions decide the answer and how far each can move before the answer flips. |
| [So-What Test](so-what-test.md) | method | A repeated question applied to a finding: "So what for this decision?" Each answer becomes the input for the next round, until the answer names an implication or an action for the client. |
| [Triangulation](triangulation.md) | method | Checking a key claim or number with two or more approaches that could have failed independently: different data, different methods, different people. |
| [Steering Committee and Final Presentation](steerco-and-final-presentation.md) | process | Get a decision, not applause. |
| [Storyline and Ghost Deck](storyline.md) | process | Write the final document's story before the analysis is done, as a sequence of slide titles with empty or sketched bodies. |
| [Synthesis](synthesis.md) | process | Turn a pile of findings into a few conclusions and one recommendation the decision maker can act on. |
