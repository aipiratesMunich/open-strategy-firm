# Deck: Which growth moves should Alder fund in 2027?

*Fictional example. Company, figures and people are invented.*

**Opening (SCQA)**
- Situation: Alder Pumps holds a strong position in Central European industrial pumps.
- Complication: Revenue has stayed at about EUR 180m for three years while the board targets EUR 200m by 2030.
- Question: Which growth moves should the board fund in 2027?
- Answer: Fund a service-contract offer and raise list prices by 4%; do not enter Poland before 2029.

**Key line**
1. The uncovered installed base is the largest untapped revenue pool.
2. List prices can rise 4% and still sit below the two main competitors.
3. Poland is not worth entering before 2029.

## 2. Alder should launch a service-contract offer and raise list prices by 4%; together they lift revenue to about EUR 199.5m, close to the EUR 200m target for 2030
layout: summary
tracker: Executive summary
lead: Alder Pumps holds a strong position in Central European industrial pumps, but revenue has stayed at about EUR 180m for three years while the board targets EUR 200m by 2030.
body:
- 31,200 installed pumps (52% of the base) are older than eight years and have no service contract.
- At 40% take-up and EUR 1,000 per pump per year, service contracts add about EUR 12.5m a year by 2030 (range EUR 7.8m to 15.6m) for an investment of EUR 6m.
- List prices are 6% below the two main competitors; a 4% increase adds about EUR 7m a year and keeps Alder below both.
- Two local players hold 78% of the Polish market; we recommend not entering before 2029.
ask: **Decision requested in March 2027:** approve EUR 6m for the service offer (owner: head of service, start April 2027) and the 4% list price increase from July 2027. The answer changes if service take-up stays below 25%.
source: Alder service model v3; distributor price lists Q2 2026; Polish market-share data 2025
note: Read the bold line and the ask. Everything after this page proves one of the four points.
